Hello!

I'm a PhD student in Computer Science at Michigan State University where I study Explainable Formal Methods.

I work on Automated Reasoning and Machine Learning using the tools of Formal Logic, Modal Logic, Model Checking, and Formal Verification.

I am interested in XAI and Interpretability as well as AI Safety and Alignment.

My goal is to understand cognition, intelligence, and reasoning through the process of creating mathematical models.
