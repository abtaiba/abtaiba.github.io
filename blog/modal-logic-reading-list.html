A modal logic reading list

Modal LogicReading
  • Bjorndahl, A. (2024). An introduction to classical and modal logics: The outlines of knowledge. Cambridge University Press.
  • Blackburn, P., de Rijke, M., & Venema, Y. (2001). Modal logic (Cambridge Tracts in Theoretical Computer Science, Vol. 53). Cambridge University Press.
  • Anderson, A. R., & Belnap, N. D. (1975–1992). Entailment: The logic of relevance and necessity (Vols. 1–2). Princeton University Press.
