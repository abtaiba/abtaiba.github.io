~/about/bio.mdmarkdown

I'm interested in understanding automated reasoning, formal verification (autoformalization), and ensuring soundness in programming languages. My background is rooted in pure mathematics and philosophy.

I got into formal verification and programming languages during my time at Carnegie Mellon University, where I got the opportunity to study Logic. Some of the classes I took that sparked my interest were Category Theory, Categorical Logic, and Interactive Theorem Proving in Lean 4. My interest in Type Theory and Learning Theory soon expanded to Programming Languages, Machine Learning, and AI.

I was a research assistant at the TART Lab, Michigan State University, where I worked on metaprogramming and debugging with a causal lens. I learnt a lot about runtime verification, programming languages, automated program repair, explainable AI, and program synthesis there.

I am also interested in AI Safety, explainability, interpretability, and alignment in general. I got interested in AI Safety following a small conference at CMU. I tried to learn more about it by taking a BlueDot course, followed by research as part of SPAR projects. I am actively looking to work on more projects in this area and am open to collaborations.
